# Legal, privacy, and health product review

Reviewed October 12, 2026. Acceptance document version: `2026-10-12-mobile-1`.

This is an engineering and product review supported by primary sources. It is not a licensed legal opinion, a professional psychology assessment, a regulatory classification decision, or certification by CFP, ANPD, FDA, Apple, or Google. No human legal or clinical consultation has been commissioned through this work.

## Scope and decisions

The planned launch is for adults in **Brazil and the United States**, on the web, iOS, and Android. English is the primary interface and resource language. EU/EEA/UK launch is outside this review. International educational links do not expand the launch market or make foreign healthcare services available in Brazil. Store availability and marketing should reflect the agreed countries; English content alone does not limit legal jurisdiction.

Anew offers educational self-care, planning, and personal reflection. It does not offer diagnosis, psychological testing, clinical scores, treatment, psychotherapy, medication advice, or emergency monitoring. The app's actual features, output, store copy, screenshots, and advertising must preserve this scope. A disclaimer cannot cure a contradictory clinical claim. A subjective rating must stay a self-observation, not become a diagnostic threshold. The onboarding is introductory and optional, does not collect clinical history, and keeps its choices in memory until registration; its quotations do not imply author endorsement or clinical validation.

Four initially unchecked confirmations apply to registration: adult declaration, Terms of Use agreement, health acknowledgement, and Privacy Notice acknowledgement. They are separate from optional AI permission and optional spiritual content. The age declaration does not verify identity or exclude every minor. No birth date or identity document is collected for this version.

Public documents in English are `/termos`, `/termos#saude`, and `/privacidade`; the existing paths are retained for compatible web/mobile links. The acknowledgement version and date are centralized in `lib/legal.ts`; Firestore's three version checks were changed at the same time. Material changes require a coordinated new version and renewed acknowledgements. For the Brazil release, validate that disclosures and instructions are understandable to the intended users; assess the need for a Portuguese translation rather than treating English text as a waiver of Brazilian rights.

Guides, journals, preferences, and saved resources remain in browser/webview local storage. Authentication is separate and does not add app-level local encryption or cloud backup. Exporting a file through the system share sheet is a user-directed disclosure to the chosen destination. Device backups, restoration, uninstall behavior, and temporary-file cleanup must be tested on both native platforms. The prepared iOS app sets and reads back backup exclusion on its Library and Documents directories before creating the WebView, and reapplies it at lifecycle transitions. Failure opens a native retry screen instead of the journal. Automatic main-storyboard creation was removed to preserve this ordering. This code is not a device-validated exclusion guarantee: macOS compilation and physical backup/restore checks remain required, including filesystem operations that can reset the attribute. See `MOBILE.md` for the procedure and Apple references.

Only `users/{uid}/settings/legal` is permitted in Firestore. The five fields are `usageTermsVersion`, `privacyVersion`, `healthVersion`, `adultConfirmed`, and `acceptedAt`. Client writes require exact fields, current versions, `adultConfirmed: true`, and `serverTimestamp()` equal to Firestore `request.time`. The owner can get, create, update, and delete this document; every other path is denied. Registration can record acknowledgements before email verification. Protected APIs independently validate token, verified email, and current acceptance metadata. See `FIRESTORE-SECURITY-AUDIT.md` for the actual verification limits.

Account deletion has now been implemented in the app's privacy controls and `/delete-account`: reauthenticate, stop pending local writes, delete the acceptance document, delete the Firebase Auth account, and attempt cleanup for that UID on the current device. This is a sequential workflow, not an atomic transaction. A failure after deleting acceptance metadata leaves protected access limited and must allow retry. A blocked local cleanup needs an explicit warning. Other devices, downloaded exports, operating-system backups, and already submitted provider data are separate. No real Firebase deletion has been verified because no project configuration is supplied. The external page still needs a deployed public URL.

AI remains disabled by default pending release review. Optional requests need a reviewed payload, specific purpose, and explicit permission per action. Keep prompts impersonal: no names, health details, beliefs, intimate accounts, confidential information, or other people's data. Provider unpaid terms expressly prohibit submitting personal, sensitive, or confidential information; a checkbox does not override those terms. A key does not establish a paid plan. Do not equate removed names with anonymization. AI and spiritual preferences must not be conditions for using manual features.

## Brazil

[LGPD, consolidated statute](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709.htm) and [ANPD's official FAQ](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/perguntas-frequentes) distinguish purpose-specific legal bases, sensitive information, transparent notices, and data rights. Account delivery and acceptance evidence may involve contract performance and exercise of rights; validate the final processing matrix rather than making consent cover all uses. Health and religious information need an Article 11 analysis. An educational app should not assume the healthcare exception applies. Identify the controller and contact, establish rights handling, minimize data, and assess international transfers and incident obligations before release.

The [Consumer Defense Code](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l8078compilado.htm) requires clear information and addresses misleading advertising and abusive contractual provisions. Do not claim guaranteed recovery, clinically validated effectiveness without suitable evidence, or blanket exemption from liability. Mandatory rights are preserved in the public terms.

The national professional body is the **Conselho Federal de Psicologia (CFP)**. [CFP's account of Resolution 7/2023](https://site.cfp.org.br/cfp-publica-resolucao-sobre-laicidade-no-exercicio-da-psicologia/) supports respect for religious and nonreligious perspectives in professional practice. Anew uses optional, explicitly enabled spiritual content as a product choice; this is not CFP approval of the app. [SATEPSI](https://satepsi.cfp.org.br/) concerns professional psychological instruments. Do not convert reflection exercises into psychological tests, reproduce restricted test items, invent scoring validation, or imply professional assessment.

[ANVISA's SaMD guidance and regularization information](https://www.gov.br/anvisa/pt-br/assuntos/noticias-anvisa/2023/novos-assuntos-para-regularizacao-de-softwares-samd-confira) contextualize software with medical purposes. If diagnostic, treatment, or disease-management features or claims are added, commission a product-specific regulatory assessment. This review does not declare a definitive medical-device exemption.

## United States federal and state context

**Claims and privacy:** The [FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance) addresses both express and implied health claims and their substantiation. Public promises must match the shipped product and data flows. Avoid describing local storage as inaccessible to the operator or others in absolute terms, or implying that login encrypts notes.

**HIPAA:** [HHS's covered entity and business associate guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/covered-entities/index.html) makes applicability depend on entity roles and relationships. A direct-to-consumer wellness app is not automatically HIPAA-regulated merely because a user enters health information. Conversely, future provider/insurer arrangements can change that assessment. Do not claim HIPAA certification or conclude that absence of HIPAA means absence of privacy obligations.

**Health breaches:** The [FTC's current HBNR compliance guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-ftcs-health-breach-notification-rule-0) describes the 2024 amendments, covered personal health records with the technical capacity to draw from multiple sources, related entities, and unauthorized disclosures as well as security intrusions. Applicability needs an actual data/function assessment; current absence of wearable integration is not a complete exemption analysis. Maintain an incident response plan that considers federal and state notice rules. The [September 2026 withdrawal](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/09/ftc-withdraws-obsolete-policy-statement) rescinded the obsolete **2021 policy statement**, not the 2024 amended rule. Do not cite that withdrawn statement as the operative standard or describe the current rule as repealed.

**Intended use:** [FDA's January 2026 General Wellness guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/general-wellness-policy-low-risk-devices) discusses low-risk healthy-lifestyle products and software unrelated to disease diagnosis, treatment, prevention, or cure. This is useful context for intended scope, not clearance or a definitive classification of Anew. Review actual functionality and claims before expanding scope or asserting non-device status in a store listing.

**Washington:** [My Health My Data, RCW 19.373](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=19.373&full=true), can reach non-HIPAA consumer health information and small businesses within its scope. Assess mental-health inferences, collection/sharing, processors, and covered consumers; do not assume startup status creates an exemption. When applicable, implement the required health-data policy link, purpose-specific collection permission or applicable necessity, separate sharing permission where required, withdrawal, deletion propagation, and request/appeal processes. General signup acknowledgements do not meet these requirements on their own. Do not launch health-data processing for covered users with an unfinished notice and rights mechanism.

**California:** [California DOJ's CCPA overview](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa) describes applicability thresholds and access, correction, deletion, opt-out, and sensitive-information rights. Assess the operator's business model and statutory thresholds before claiming applicability or an exemption. The current absence of ad/analytics SDKs does not dispense with that assessment. This is not a complete 50-state analysis: identify every launch state's applicable consumer health, general privacy, breach, and consumer protection rules with qualified US counsel before national distribution. Add local rights/appeal mechanisms where needed; do not publish a fictitious comprehensive state-rights section.

## Apple and Google release requirements

| Area | Release action and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Account deletion | Test the implemented in-app deletion flow end to end and publish `/delete-account` on an accessible web host. Explain partial failures and lawful retention. Do not substitute sign-out or local clearing for account deletion. |
| Privacy disclosures | Publish final operator/contact/retention notices at accessible HTTPS URLs; reconcile App Store privacy answers and Google Data safety with actual Firebase SDK, server, export, and optional AI behavior. Audit native SDKs, permission prompts, privacy manifests, and backup settings. |
| Health content | Use an accurate Google Health apps declaration, keep claims educational, include appropriate health disclaimers and professional-care reminders, and obtain a specific regulatory assessment before a non-device assertion or clinical feature. |
| Optional AI | Keep disabled until plan/contracts are reviewed. Verify explicit third-party disclosure/permission. Assess Google AI policy scope and implement in-app reporting and moderation if required; do not ship a fake reporting button with no handling process. |
| Distribution | Limit store countries and marketing to Brazil/US, complete age ratings honestly, verify app functionality on real devices, and provide working reviewer access without real private health data. Store approval has not occurred. |

[Apple account deletion guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/guideline-reference/5-1-1-account-deletion/) requires initiation within account-creating apps and removal of associated data except lawful retention; ordinary apps should not force a support call/email. [Google's deletion guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en) additionally requires an external working deletion request pathway, named for the app/developer, accessible to people who removed the app.

[Apple Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) address health claims, reachable developer information, privacy, explicit permission for third-party AI sharing, and health-data restrictions. [Google Health Content and Services](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16679511?hl=en) requires applicable health disclosures and accurate medical-device information; [the Health declaration instructions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14738291?hl=en) apply even to developers declaring no health functionality. [Google AI content policy and scope](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14094294?hl=en) distinguish generative apps from certain limited productivity uses; determine applicability to the final feature set before launch. Broader safety and privacy policies still apply.
